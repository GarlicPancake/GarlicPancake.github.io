A 64x32 LED matrix display that shows the next three train arrivals for a Metro station. A MatrixPortal M4 polls a small proxy server that fetches data from the WMATA API, then sorts trains by arrival time and shortens destination names to fit the screen. A corner status dot shows green for live data and red when the board is showing cached data. If the connection drops, it holds the last good result for 70 seconds. I fixed flicker by lowering brightness to reduce current spikes, only redrawing text when it changes, and creating the display once at startup. Tools: Adafruit MatrixPortal M4, CircuitPython, Python, WMATA API, REST/JSON
